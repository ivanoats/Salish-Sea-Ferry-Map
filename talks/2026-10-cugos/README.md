# CUGOS lightning talk, October 2026

*One Sea, 23 Ferry Operators, Zero Maps.* A 20-slide Ignite talk (15 seconds a slide).

- `salish-sea-ferry-ignite.html`: the deck. Self-contained reveal.js; open it in a browser. Press **S** for speaker notes and **A** to pause auto-advance.
- `assets/`: Salish Sea illustration experiments built from the design elements of Coast Salish art (circle, crescent, extended crescent, trigon). They are inspired by those elements, not Coast Salish art, and are not for sale or merchandise.
